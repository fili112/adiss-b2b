Adigrat University  |  College of Engineering & Technology  |  Department of Software Engineering  |  2025/2026

Project Summary

A production-grade, localized enterprise B2B e-commerce platform built for Ethiopian wholesale distributors.

Replaces informal trade — WhatsApp negotiations, paper invoices, and cash receipts — with a secure, governed digital procurement ecosystem.

Enforces corporate financial controls, supports bulk price negotiation (RFQ), and delivers omnichannel access via Web and Mobile.

All transactions are processed exclusively in Ethiopian Birr (ETB / "Br") — eliminating foreign currency friction.
