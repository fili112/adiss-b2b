- ✅ **Requires Admin Approval**
- ✅ **Requires Sales Manager Approval**

```

    ↓ Final review & approval

    ↓ Fulfillment begins
```

### Scenario: Spending Limit Block

> "Let's say Kebede tries to order 15 laptops worth 1,948,500 ETB."

**Check limits**:
- Employee limit: 100,000 ETB ❌
- Company limit: 500,000 ETB ❌
- **Result**: Blocked, needs override

> "This prevents unauthorized spending and keeps budgets in check."

### Scenario: Admin Override

> "Alem, as company admin, can approve it if it's legitimate."

**Admin approves**:
- Increases Kebede's limit temporarily
- Or takes over the order himself
- Order proceeds with proper authorization

> "Complete control over who spends what, when, and how much."
