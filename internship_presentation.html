Problem Analysis

Wholesale Commerce Challenges in Ethiopia

Why generic global e-commerce platforms fail in the Ethiopian enterprise context

ETHIO-B2B // PROBLEM
⚠️
Foreign Currency & Pricing Friction
Off-the-shelf platforms default to USD or EUR with forced multi-currency setups, causing accounting mismatches, banking complications, and confusing exchange rates for local buyers.
❌ Result: Local distributors abandon platforms in favor of manual phone calls and spreadsheet invoices.
📉
Lack of B2B Corporate Governance
B2C e-commerce assumes a single credit card holder. It lacks spending limits, purchase requisition approvals, employee permission trees, and multi-tier volume pricing.
❌ Result: Company managers cannot safely delegate purchasing power to regional branch staff.
📑
Manual Quotation & Negotiation Delays
High-volume wholesale trades require custom quote negotiations (RFQ) rather than fixed shelf prices. Without digital RFQ tooling, transactions take days via email and WhatsApp.
❌ Result: Slow quotation cycles and loss of high-value wholesale accounts.
📶
Mobile Access & Field Readiness
Retail store owners in Merkato and regional hubs operate on mobile devices rather than office desktop computers. They need dedicated native apps to check wholesale stock.
❌ Result: Desktop-only web stores lose significant market share in the Ethiopian retail landscape.

Core B2B Innovation 3

B2B Quote Negotiation Engine (RFQ)

Digital price negotiation for high-volume enterprise transactions

ETHIO-B2B // QUOTES
💬
The RFQ Lifecycle
Empowers business customers to negotiate custom bulk pricing without leaving the digital platform:
  • Quote Submission: Buyer requests a quote for 100x 34" Monitors (List: 5,990,000 ETB) with custom notes: "Ordering for university lab upgrade in Hawassa".
  • Distributor Counter-Offer: Sales team reviews inventory in Addis Ababa warehouse and responds with volume discount of 5,400,000 ETB.
  • Acceptance & Conversion: Customer accepts the counter-offer with one click, automatically generating a binding B2B order with locked prices.
📊
Technical Advantages of Custom RFQ
Built directly into Medusa v2 custom link architecture:
  • Inventory Reservation: Temporarily holds stock during active negotiation window to prevent overselling.
  • Audit History: Preserves full messaging and negotiation timestamp logs between distributor and client.
  • Mobile Ready: Quotes can be created, negotiated, and accepted directly from the mobile app on iOS and Android.

Problem Solving

Technical Challenges & Solutions

Overcoming architectural and integration hurdles during development

ETHIO-B2B // SOLUTIONS
⚙️
Challenge 1: Medusa v2 Module Linking
Issue: Medusa v2 uses a strict micro-kernel link system. Associating Company entities with Customer Accounts and Approval limits required custom link definitions.
Solution: Leveraged Medusa's Remote Link engine to establish decoupled joins between `company_id` and `customer_id` without database lockups.
🔄
Challenge 2: Approval State Transitions
Issue: Ensuring carts blocked by spending limits do not get charged before admin authorization, while maintaining inventory reservation.
Solution: Created an intermediate `REQUIRES_APPROVAL` state that holds cart line items in draft without triggering payment gateway captures.
💱
Challenge 3: Hardcoded Multi-Currency Defaults
Issue: Default Medusa seed scripts contained multiple foreign currencies (EUR, USD) that appeared in dropdowns.
Solution: Refactored the entire `seed.ts` pipeline, deleted non-ETB price sets, and configured ETB as the store's exclusive default currency.
